Property Description

Lot & Layout

40 x 110 ft lot featuring ~1,754 sq. ft. above-grade space with 3 bedrooms & 2 full baths upstairs, plus a versatile finished basement.

Basement Potential

Finished basement with separate entrance, 3 rooms (used as bedrooms), full bathroom, kitchen & laundry for extended family or rental use.

Prime Location

Transit directly in front, close to schools, shopping, and restaurants. Just a 10-minute drive to Ajax GO Station.

Welcome to 46 Hearne Cres, a spacious and lovingly maintained home offering an impressive and versatile layout in a highly convenient Ajax location. Situated on a 40 x 110 ft lot, this approximately 1,754 sq. ft. above-grade home features 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms on the second floor, with additional finished basement space offering 3 rooms currently used as bedrooms, a full bathroom, separate kitchen, laundry and a separate entrance.

The main floor offers an ideal layout for both everyday living and entertaining, with separate living and dining rooms, plus a dedicated family room providing additional privacy and a comfortable space to relax or entertain. A convenient powder room completes the main level. The spacious kitchen features a large window overlooking the breakfast area, allowing abundant natural sunlight to brighten the space. Generous windows throughout the home create a bright and welcoming atmosphere.

The finished basement provides valuable flexibility for extended family, guests, recreation, a home office or other personal uses. With its separate entrance, kitchen and laundry facilities, it also offers potential for additional living or rental use, subject to applicable municipal requirements.

Recent improvements include an upgrade to a 200-amp electrical service (2023), basement washer and dryer (2023), and main-floor washer (2024). The home also features a 2-car garage with parking for an additional 2 vehicles on the driveway.

Location is a true highlight. Public transit is conveniently located directly in front of the home, while schools, shopping, restaurants and everyday amenities are all within easy reach. Ajax GO Station is approximately a 10-minute drive away, and numerous retail and service options are conveniently located nearby.

Meticulously cared for by the current owners, this home reflects a true pride of ownership. From the thoughtful layout to the well-maintained interiors, the care and attention given to the property are evident throughout. A rare opportunity to own a spacious and versatile home in a highly convenient Ajax location.
